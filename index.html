About TIP

A community built on learning by doing.

The Icaris Project is a youth-powered initiative where technology, business, design, and innovation come together. Founded by students, for students, TIP empowers young people to explore emerging fields, develop real-world skills, and build projects that create meaningful impact.

Unlike traditional educational organizations, we emphasize doing over listening. Through hands-on programs, collaborative experiences, and creative problem-solving, students gain the confidence to turn ambitious ideas into reality — without waiting for permission to start.

Mission

Inspire and empower.

We provide accessible opportunities to learn, create, collaborate, and lead across technology, business, and design — for the next generation of innovators.

Vision

A global community of creators.

We're building a network of young people who use technology and innovation to solve meaningful problems and shape a better future.

Why TIP

Not another program to sit through.

Student‑led

TIP is run by the students in it. Every program, event, and project reflects what this generation actually wants to build — not a curriculum handed down from above.

By students, for students

Collaborative

You won't build alone. TIP pairs curious people with different strengths — coders with designers, strategists with builders — so ideas get stress-tested before they ship.

Cross-disciplinary by design

Project‑based

We measure progress in things built, not hours attended. Every track ends with something real: a product, a pitch, a portfolio piece, a launched project.

Learning by doing

Impact‑focused

We push past "just for practice." TIP projects are built to matter — for the community, for a real user, or for the problem you actually care about solving.
